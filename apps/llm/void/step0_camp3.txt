TODAY: Year 1, autumn, day 5 of 10. Winter in 5 days. Whatever is still in a field on winter's first day is lost to the frost.

PEOPLE: 3 left: Dirk De Wit, Marit Wever and Bente Veen, all housed. 2 working: Dirk cuts wood, Bente carries water. Marit has no work. 10 have died, 9 of thirst and 1 of hunger; the last was Thijs Wever, a baby, 4 days ago.

STORES: food for 2 days. Water 2, and Bente is carrying more. Wood 60. No provisions, no rope.

FIELDS: none.

THREATS: none seen.
