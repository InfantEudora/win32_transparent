# Important ElevenLabs Voice Direction

For the actual **TTS generation**, I'd use a consistent instruction before generating the clips:

> **Female fantasy archer companion. Warm, confident and natural. Experienced but approachable. Speaks conversationally, never like a formal narrator. Slightly dry sense of humor. Calm during danger. Quietly enthusiastic when the player succeeds. Medium-low energy, clear articulation, believable pauses. She is physically present in the game world and is speaking directly to the player.**

Then generate the individual lines separately.

For example:

**ARCHER_015**

> Steady... breathe... now.

**ARCHER_016**

> Nice shot.

This should give you much better usable game assets than generating a 5-minute continuous narration, because your engine can then do things like:

```text
PLAYER_ENTERS_LEVEL
    → ARCHER_001

PLAYER_MOVES
    → ARCHER_004

PLAYER_REACHES_GAP
    → ARCHER_005

PLAYER_FAILS_JUMP
    → ARCHER_008

PLAYER_DISCOVERS_BOW
    → ARCHER_010

PLAYER_AIMS_AT_TARGET
    → ARCHER_014

TARGET_HIT
    → ARCHER_015

TARGET_KNOCKED_DOWN
    → ARCHER_024

PLAYER_DISCOVERS_ROPE
    → ARCHER_031

PLAYER_SWINGS
    → ARCHER_035

PLAYER_RELEASES_ROPE
    → ARCHER_037

LANDING_SUCCESS
    → ARCHER_038

ENTER_CASTLE
    → ARCHER_040
```

That structure would fit your engine particularly well because **the narration becomes another reactive game system rather than a linear cutscene**. You can also add cooldowns and random selection between equivalent lines so she doesn't repeat herself when the player retries an obstacle.
