Chasm's defence groundwork was talked through and agreed with the user on 2026-10-09, written up as
`apps/chasm/docs/defence_plan.md`. Steps 1-2 BUILT and tested 2026-10-09 (uncommitted at the time; see the plan's
"As built"); step 3, gate doors, is next; an ordered load is LOST until the crates plan (another window) lands. Build order: 1 orders (right-click a selected person
to post him; a post is his job; ordered people are out of `AssignJobs`), 2 the pack, 3 gate doors (shut
for everyone; independent - can go to a worker window), 4 site costs per good, 5 provisions (new good,
own bit OUTSIDE `GOODS_FOOD`; goods masks go to 16 bits), 6 guard tower, 7 caretakers resupply towers,
8 right-click on a tower.

**Why:** the user wants defence (P6, zombies) built on people who can be sent somewhere and stay, without
Knights-and-Merchants-style feeding chores - supply is automatic, placement is the player's part.

**How to apply:** read the plan before touching orders, the tower, provisions or gates in chasm. Numbers
in it are tuning ([[tuning-numbers-not-rules]]); balance stocks at 3 water : 2 provisions (water runs out
first and kills). Related: [[chasm-game-plan]], [[line-works-plan]].
